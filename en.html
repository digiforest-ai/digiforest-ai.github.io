About DigiForest

Small steps.
Real results.

DigiForest is a DX and AI advisory run by Kyoko Yamanaka, based in Uji, Kyoto. We help restaurants, beauty salons, hotels, nightlife venues, and care facilities take their first — and next — steps into digital transformation.

Our approach is practical: no large-scale system overhauls, just the right tools for the right problems, starting with what businesses already have. From free diagnostics to hands-on implementation support, we stay involved through the whole process.

As an authorized distributor of the JTBC DX Series, we also work with regional and international partners to expand access to these Japan-proven tools.
